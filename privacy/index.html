Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Operator: Oleg Pyshenkin

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RoamXP (“RoamXP,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the RoamXP mobile application and this website. It describes current product behavior as implemented today. It is not a guarantee that every practice will remain unchanged forever.

RoamXP is designed as a local-first experience. There is currently no RoamXP user account system and no RoamXP-operated backend or cloud storage for your app content. Limited anonymous or pseudonymous product analytics are collected through PostHog, as described below.

2. Local-First Design

RoamXP is built so that your core content and activity stay on your device. RoamXP does not currently operate a RoamXP account login system, and no RoamXP backend or cloud storage for app content was found in the current implementation.

Local app data may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple and iCloud backup settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use RoamXP, the app may store locally:

  • journal text
  • journal photos
  • journal audio
  • nutrition logs
  • workout logs
  • goals
  • progression / XP
  • campaign state
  • achievements
  • onboarding state
  • step totals
  • Screen Time / Family Controls selections
  • preferences
  • entitlement/cache state

This information is used to provide RoamXP features on your device. Local app data may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple and iCloud backup settings.

4. Journal and User Content

Journal text is stored locally on your device. Journal photos and journal audio are also stored locally. RoamXP does not upload this content to a RoamXP server. This content is not sent to PostHog.

5. Apple HealthKit

RoamXP may request Apple HealthKit permission to read step count only. In the current implementation, RoamXP does not write data to HealthKit.

Step values are used locally for walking, Journey, and progression features. Raw HealthKit values are not sent to PostHog. HealthKit data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

RoamXP is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

6. Screen Time and Focus Features

For Focus and distraction-protection features, RoamXP uses Apple APIs such as FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity.

Related selections and tokens are stored locally and, where required by Apple, in Apple App Group storage. Selected app or category identities and opaque tokens are not sent to PostHog. This information is not used for advertising.

7. Product Analytics

RoamXP currently uses PostHog for limited anonymous or pseudonymous product analytics. This helps understand onboarding, feature use, and subscription-related interactions so the product can be improved.

Analytics may include:

  • onboarding progress
  • onboarding screen/step metadata
  • explicitly instrumented feature interactions
  • coarse paywall/subscription interactions
  • purchase/restore status
  • permission outcomes
  • app version
  • build number
  • platform
  • OS/device-type metadata
  • a pseudonymous PostHog installation/distinct identifier

Current PostHog configuration includes:

  • no identify() calls
  • no alias() calls
  • no group calls
  • personProfiles = never
  • session replay disabled
  • lifecycle autocapture disabled
  • surveys disabled
  • remote feature flags disabled
  • automatic error capture disabled
  • push capture disabled
  • GeoIP enrichment disabled

Network requests inherently may make an IP address transiently available to infrastructure providers, but RoamXP configures its analytics service not to retain client IP data for product analytics.

RoamXP does not send the following to PostHog:

  • name
  • email
  • journal text
  • journal photos
  • journal audio
  • raw HealthKit step values
  • Screen Time selections/tokens
  • notification body text
  • StoreKit receipts/full transaction payloads
  • precise GPS location

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store (including via expo-iap in the current implementation). RoamXP does not receive or store payment card information.

Limited subscription or product interaction metadata may be sent to PostHog. Full StoreKit receipts and full transaction payloads are not sent to PostHog.

Superwall is not integrated in the current implementation. No advertising or attribution SDK was found for purchase attribution.

9. Notifications

In the current implementation, RoamXP may request notification permission and may use local notifications or reminders. Notification title and body content is not sent to PostHog.

No verified Apple Push Notification service (APNs) push-token collection path currently exists. This describes current behavior and is not a permanent guarantee that notification handling will never change.

10. Location

RoamXP does not request native GPS or location permission. RoamXP does not collect precise GPS location. PostHog GeoIP enrichment is disabled.

11. Advertising and Tracking

No advertising SDK is currently integrated. RoamXP does not use IDFA, and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) / IDFA APIs are not used in the current implementation.

RoamXP does not use app data for cross-app advertising tracking. RoamXP does not sell personal information for advertising.

These statements describe current implementation and are not broad certifications about every possible future change.

12. Accounts

RoamXP currently does not create user accounts. No RoamXP email/password or login system currently exists.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data. Local data may be removable through available local controls in the app or on your device. Uninstalling the app removes installed local app data from the device, subject to Apple and device backup behavior. Local app data may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple and iCloud backup settings.

PostHog analytics. Analytics data may be retained according to configured analytics-provider retention. Because analytics is pseudonymous and not tied to a RoamXP account, RoamXP may not always be able to reliably associate analytics records with a real-world person.

For privacy questions or requests, contact puzdevelop@gmail.com.

14. Children

RoamXP is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

15. Website Privacy

Separately from the mobile app, the RoamXP website currently uses no RoamXP analytics SDK, no advertising pixels, and no first-party tracking cookies in the site code. The website does not contain a contact form and uses mailto links for support or contact.

Hosting and network providers may process ordinary connection metadata necessary to serve the website (for example, technical request information needed to deliver pages).

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the product, providers, or practices. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of RoamXP or this website after an update means you should review the revised policy.
