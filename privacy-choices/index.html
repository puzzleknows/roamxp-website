Privacy Choices

A clear look at the privacy controls available with RoamXP today—local data, Apple permissions, analytics, and how to reach us.

1. Your Privacy Choices

RoamXP currently has no user account or login system and no RoamXP cloud sync. Most personal and user-created content stays on your device. Your main controls are local app settings, Apple system permissions, and contacting us with privacy questions.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy. Related pages: Terms of Use and Support.

2. Local Data

Most personal and user-created data—including journal text, photos, and audio—is stored locally on your device. This content is local-first and is not uploaded to a RoamXP backend. RoamXP has no cloud account or cloud data dashboard.

Where available in the app, you can delete data using local controls. Uninstalling RoamXP removes installed local app data from that device, subject to Apple and device backup behavior.

Local app data may also be included in device or iCloud backups depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not guarantee that every copy is permanently erased from every backup.

3. Apple Permissions

You can control Apple permissions for RoamXP in iOS Settings. Where applicable, that may include:

  • Health
  • Photos
  • Microphone
  • Notifications
  • Screen Time / Family Controls

Disabling a permission may limit the related RoamXP feature. For example, Health access is used to read step count locally; Screen Time / Family Controls authorization is used for Focus features. Selected Screen Time / Family Controls identities and tokens remain local and are not sent to PostHog. Raw HealthKit step values are not sent to PostHog.

4. Product Analytics

RoamXP currently uses PostHog for limited anonymous or pseudonymous product analytics. Analytics is not tied to a RoamXP login or account. The PostHog project is configured to discard client IP data.

RoamXP does not send the following to PostHog:

  • journal content (text, photos, or audio)
  • raw HealthKit step values
  • Screen Time selections or tokens
  • notification body text
  • full StoreKit receipts
  • precise GPS location

Because analytics uses a pseudonymous installation/distinct identifier rather than a RoamXP account, RoamXP may not always be able to reliably identify which analytics events belong to a specific real-world person.

5. Advertising & Tracking

RoamXP currently does not include advertising SDKs. RoamXP does not use IDFA, does not use app data for cross-app advertising tracking, and does not sell personal information for advertising. RoamXP does not request precise GPS / location permission.

6. Privacy Requests

You may contact puzdevelop@gmail.com for:

  • privacy questions
  • requests regarding information RoamXP may hold
  • questions about analytics or local data handling

Because RoamXP currently does not have user accounts and product analytics is pseudonymous, we may not always be able to associate analytics records with a particular real-world person.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email puzdevelop@gmail.com.

You can also review the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Support pages.
